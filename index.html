Why is Stripe-managed risk unavailable?
Stripe-managed risk is only available with direct charges. When the platform creates the charge (destination/SCT), the platform must own merchant risk and loss liability.
docs.stripe.com/connect/risk-management
Why can't the connected account pay fees directly?
With destination and SCT charges, the platform creates the charge on its own account. Stripe fees are deducted from the platform. The platform controls what it charges connected accounts separately.
docs.stripe.com/connect/direct-charges
Why is revenue share unavailable?
Revenue share requires Standard Connect where the connected account pays Stripe fees directly. When the platform pays fees (buy-rate model), the platform earns margin by marking up what they charge connected accounts instead.
trailhead.corp.stripe.com/docs/spe/program-elements/revenue-share
on_behalf_of effect: Shifts the customer-facing identity (descriptor, Radar address) to the connected account. Chargeback liability, refund control, fund flow, payment method ownership, and dispute evidence remain with the platform.
SCT note: The transfer is manual and decoupled from the charge. No automatic reversal on refund. The platform has full control over transfer timing and amount.
SCT + OBO: Same shifted attributes as Destination + OBO. The difference: the transfer remains manual, decoupled from the charge, with no automatic reversal on refund.
